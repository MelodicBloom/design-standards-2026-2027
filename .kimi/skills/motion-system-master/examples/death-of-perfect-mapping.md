## Philosophy applied to motion

This deck’s thesis is that chaos only works when infrastructure remains calm. The motion system must embody that literally:
- Expressive motion (xl, lg, Organic) is reserved for hero and manifesto only — the deck’s two emotional poles
- Everything in between uses Structured motion at md or sm — controlled, clear, invisible
- The contrast between those two states *is* the argument; the motion proves the point
