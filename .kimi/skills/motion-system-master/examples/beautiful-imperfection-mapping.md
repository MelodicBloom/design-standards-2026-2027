## Zone rule (from source)

**Zone A (the riot):** hero, gallery, manifesto — deploy glitch, drift, and expressive max-density motion.
**Zone B (the calm):** technical, diagram, proof, form, nav — invisible infrastructure, no expressive motion.

The contrast between Zone A and Zone B is the system’s entire argument. Motion must model it.
